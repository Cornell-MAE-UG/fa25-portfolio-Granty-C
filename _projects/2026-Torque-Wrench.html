Baseline calculations

The M42 steel beam has a 16.0 in lever arm, a 0.500 in thickness (b), and a 0.450 in width in the bending plane (h). Its elastic modulus is 32.0 Mpsi.

F = T/L = 37.50 lbfI = bh³/12 = 0.003797 in⁴ σ = Th/(2I) = 35.56 ksiδ = FL³/(3EI) = 0.4214 in

Why stress differs. In Ansys I fixed the upper 0.400 in of the drive and loaded the end face. Stress concentrates at the fixed edge, reaching 3.19 times the nominal beam stress.

Why deflection differs. Ansys predicts 11.2% more travel because the head and exposed drive can rotate. The hand model fixes the beam at its root.

Final CAD and material

Design iteration. The 0.300 in trial beam missed the fracture target (1.93). The final profile gives 1.450 in of travel at 52.66 ksi by hand. Its safety factors are 4.18 for yield, 2.94 for fracture and 2.35 for fatigue. Full-size CAD drawings (PDF)

Custom 465 stainless steel · H950

I chose H950 for its fracture toughness of 61.4 ksi√in, compared with 15 ksi√in for M42.

Material inputs. E = 28.7 Mpsi, ν = 0.28, yield = 220 ksi, ultimate = 240 ksi. The fatigue strength is 124 ksi at 100,000 fully reversed cycles (R = −1).

The socket model meets all four course targets. With the drive faces fixed, the peak stress rises as the mesh is refined. That model gives 1.32 for yield and 0.89 for fracture. An extra fatigue check for the peened drive including mean stress gives 1.48 against 1.50, so I would test the drive in a real socket before rating it.

Predicted scale sensitivity. At 600 in·lbf, the tip travels 1.136 in across the scale, or 0.001894 in per in·lbf on average. It also shifts 0.130 in toward the head, so the graduations follow a 5.040 in radius arc.

Weight, cost and manufacture

2.080 lb assembled · $7.92 USD in finished metal. The starting steel blank costs about $34.77 before machining, heat treatment and calibration.

Machinability. Aged H950 cuts slowly (Granta reference speed 22 surface ft/min). I’d rough-machine the annealed blank, age it, then finish and peen the drive.

Recycling and corrosion. The H950 body and 304 tube are recyclable once separated from the aluminum indicator parts. H950 resists fresh water well. Granta lists seawater service as restricted.

Reflection

What would matter in a tool I sold?

I’d check whether an oily glove slips and whether the pointer is readable at an angle. A replaceable pointer would let someone repair a dropped wrench.

What would I need to learn?

I’d need to quantify calibration uncertainty, measure how hand position affects the reading, and price heat treatment and inspection for a small batch.

Who could teach me?

I’d review the drawing with a machinist and heat treater, then work with a calibration technician to apply known torques in both directions and compare sockets.

How would I keep learning?

I’ve used beam wrenches and always liked that the only movement comes from the material flexing. Seeing how much the socket changed my stress prediction reminded me why I need to test my assumptions. On future builds I’d record a prediction and check it against a measurement.
